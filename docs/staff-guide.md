# Using the booking system

A short guide for everyone working front of house. Everything here works on a
phone.

---

## Signing in

Go to **irmak-restaurant.com/admin** and sign in with the email and password you
were given.

You will stay signed in on that phone, so you should only need to do this once.
If you forget your password, ask whoever set the system up — there is no reset
link, on purpose.

**Do not share your login.** Everyone who needs access gets their own.

---

## The bookings screen

This is what opens when you sign in, and it is where you will spend all your time.

At the top you see the two numbers that matter:

- **Bookings** — how many tables are booked
- **Covers** — how many people are coming

Cancelled bookings are not counted in either, but they still show in the list,
greyed out, in case the guest turns up anyway.

Use the **arrows** either side of the date to move a day at a time, or the date
box to jump to any day. If you have moved off today, a **Back to today** link
appears under the date.

Each row shows the time, the number of people, and the name. A **gold bookmark**
on the right means there is a note to read — an allergy, a birthday, an access
need. Always open those before service.

Tap any row to open it.

---

## Opening a booking

You get the guest's full name, party size, time, and any note they left.

- Tap **Call** to ring them. It dials straight away.
- Tap **Email** to email them.

Anything the guest typed themselves appears in a **gold box**. You cannot edit
that, deliberately — it is a record of what they actually told us.

### Marking what happened

Three buttons: **Arrived**, **No show**, **Cancelled**.

Tap one and it is saved immediately. If you tap the wrong one, tap the
highlighted button again and it goes back to Confirmed.

Please mark **Arrived** as people are seated. It is what makes the "last visit"
column on the customer list mean anything.

### Changing a booking

Under **Change the booking** you can adjust the party size and the time. This
ignores the capacity limits — if you decide the table works, the system will not
argue.

### Internal notes

The **Internal note** box is staff-only. The guest never sees it and it is never
emailed. Use it for things like "regular, likes the window table" or "arrived 40
minutes late last time".

---

## Taking a booking over the phone

Tap **Add booking** — it is always at the bottom of the bookings screen.

Fill in the date, time, party size and the guest's details. You need an email
address for the confirmation, so ask for one.

Two things worth knowing:

- The online limits **do not apply**. You can book 30 people for tomorrow
  lunchtime if you want to.
- If the slot is already full, you will get a message saying so. Tick **Allow
  over capacity** and save again to book it anyway. Only do that when you know
  the kitchen can handle it — the system trusts you completely here.

---

## Customers

The **Customers** tab lists everyone who has ever booked, most frequent first.

Search by name, email or phone. Phone search works whichever way the number was
written — `07700 900123` finds `+447700900123`.

Tick **Marketing opt-in only** to see just the people who agreed to receive
emails.

### Exporting for an offer or giveaway

Tap **Export CSV** and it downloads a spreadsheet you can open in Excel. It
includes a **Marketing opt-in** column and the date each person agreed.

> **Only ever email the people whose opt-in says Yes.** Emailing the others is
> against the law, not just bad manners. If you tick "Marketing opt-in only"
> before exporting, the file only contains people you are allowed to email.

### If a guest asks to be deleted

People have a legal right to have their data removed. Find them, tap **Delete**,
and confirm.

This removes them and **all of their bookings, including past ones**, and cannot
be undone. Only do it when someone has actually asked.

---

## Settings

Mostly for the manager.

- **Booking rules** — the biggest party that can book online, how much notice
  guests need to give, and how far ahead they can book.
- **Opening hours and capacity** — how many covers you will take per time slot.
  Read the note on that screen before changing it; the cap and the interval only
  make sense together.
- **Closures and private hire** — block a whole day or part of one. Guests
  cannot book it, but **you still can** using Add booking.
- **Venue details** — name, phone, address and the address that booking
  notifications go to. These appear on the booking page and in every
  confirmation email.

---

## What guests get

When someone books online, two emails go out immediately: a confirmation to the
guest, and a notification to the restaurant with all the details and any notes.

The guest's confirmation has a **cancel link**. If they use it, the booking turns
to Cancelled on your screen and the table is freed automatically. That is
deliberate — it saves you fielding cancellation calls mid-service.

---

## If something looks wrong

- **A booking is missing** — check the date at the top. It is easy to be looking
  at the wrong day.
- **A guest says they booked but you cannot find them** — search the Customers
  tab by their phone number or email.
- **Nothing loads** — check the phone has signal, then reload. If it is still
  broken, take bookings on paper and call whoever set the system up. Nothing is
  lost.
